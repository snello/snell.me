# About
First and foremost, I am a human-being living in New York City with my family.

I was born in the United Kingdom and grew up with a curiosity for how things work, that led to many disassembled items around the house; from He-Man toys to binoculars, but always a growth in understanding as well as an expanded idea of what is possible.

This naturally led me to computers, the programs of which I found I could more easily dis/assemble without fear of any familial repurcussions!

After school at 18, I opted to apply for an apprenticeship with [BT Labs](https://atadastral.co.uk/bt/) over heading directly off to University. Specifically, I joined the the Satellite Communications group working on improving the operation and monitoring of BTs Satellite Earth stations. Space, the final frontier! I had finally made it. 🚀 The team I was assigned to were a fantastic group with their head in the clouds, exactly where I wanted to be. I wrote-up (more for my own memory) a little bit about the biggest project I tackled during my apprenticeship [here](/2022/06/03/Apprenticeship-at-BT-Labs/).

Having completed my apprenticeship, I set out to gain a deeper understanding of technology at the [University of East Anglia](https://www.uea.ac.uk/) gaining a BSc in Computer Science. My final year project was working with the [R language](https://www.r-project.org/) to implement, test and improve on the various low-bandwidth voice encoding standards in use on mobile networks at the time.
